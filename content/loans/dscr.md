## Who are DSCR loans for?

- **Investors growing a rental portfolio** who've outgrown conventional limits
- **Self-employed investors** whose tax returns don't show their real income
- **Investors whose debt-to-income is maxed out** from other properties
- **Anyone buying in an LLC**
- **Out-of-state investors** buying in California, Texas, Florida, or Colorado

DSCR loans are for **investment properties only**, not the home you live in.

## How does a DSCR loan work?

We compare the property's **monthly rent** with its **full monthly payment** (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and HOA dues). If the rent covers the payment, the property qualifies on its own. No tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs. [How to calculate DSCR →](/answers/how-to-calculate-dscr)

## What does a DSCR loan cost?

I'll be straight with you:

- **Higher rates** than conventional investment loans, because DSCR loans can't be sold to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac
- **Down payment** of about 20% to 25% on most purchases
- **Prepayment penalties** are common, often for the first one to five years. I'll show you the penalty options in dollars. [How prepayment penalties work →](/answers/dscr-loan-prepayment-penalty)
- **Closing costs** commonly 2% to 5% of the loan amount, plus any points you choose
- **Reserves:** usually 3 to 12 months of payments left in the bank after closing

## DSCR or conventional?

If your income is easy to document and your debt-to-income has room, a conventional investment loan usually costs less. DSCR wins when your tax returns understate your income, you're near the 10-property limit, or you want an LLC. We offer both. ## Example scenario (illustrative)

An investor in Phoenix already owns eight rentals and lives in California. She finds a duplex near Austin for $420,000 with $3,600 in total rent. With 25% down and Texas property taxes, the full payment is about $3,150, a ratio of 1.14. She closes in her LLC without showing a single tax return, and uses a DSCR cash-out refinance on an older rental to fund her next down payment.
