Open-ended questions get the clearest picture of what a client really wants. Pick two or three from each group, and listen more than you talk. Home equity only comes up if the answers point there.

## Feeling secure about what's next

- How do you feel about keeping up your current lifestyle for the long run?
- How would you handle a big, unexpected home or health expense?
- How confident are you about staying in your home as you get older?
- How would it feel to have a line of credit set aside for emergencies?
- How do you feel about your budget right now?

## Taking care of the here and now

- What would you change about your home if money weren't a concern?
- Who do you spend your time with, and who could you call for help?
- What would make it easier to see family and friends more often?

## Finding purpose

- What activities bring you the most joy?
- What role do you play in your family or community?
- What have you done recently that you're proud of?

## Reaching the goals that matter

- If you could design your dream retirement, what would it look like?
- What's still on your bucket list?
- When you think about the legacy you want to leave, what comes to mind first?
- What could you do for your family now that would make a lasting difference?

## When the answers point to home equity

Listen for: a mortgage payment that strains the budget, worry about selling investments in a bad market, big goals with no clear way to fund them, or a strong wish to stay in the home.
