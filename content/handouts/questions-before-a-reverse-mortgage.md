Ask any lender these questions, including us. A good lender will answer every one in plain English and in writing.

1. **Is this an FHA-insured HECM or a private (proprietary) reverse mortgage?** The protections and costs are different.
2. **What are all the costs?** Ask for the FHA insurance premium, origination fee, closing costs, and ongoing costs, on paper.
3. **How much can I get, and in what form?** A lump sum, monthly payments, a line of credit, or a mix.
4. **Will the unused line of credit grow, and how?**
5. **What happens to my current mortgage?** A HECM pays it off; a reverse mortgage second can keep it.
6. **What are my ongoing obligations?** Taxes, insurance, HOA dues, upkeep, and living in the home.
7. **What protects my spouse,** especially if they're under 62 or not on the loan?
8. **What happens when I pass away,** and what are my heirs' options and deadlines?
9. **Will anyone ask me to buy an annuity, insurance, or investment with this money?** The answer should be no.
10. **Can my family and my financial professional join the calls?** The answer should be yes.

## Signs to walk away

- Pressure to sign quickly, or to skip counseling or leave family out
- Promises of "free money" or "no payments ever"
- Anyone asking you to sign over your deed
- A contractor offering to "set up the loan" to pay for their own work

## Before you apply

Meet with an **independent HUD-approved counselor** (required for a HECM), bring your family, and ask your financial professional to review the numbers alongside your plan.
