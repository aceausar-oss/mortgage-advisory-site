## What's different about a mortgage in Colorado?

- **Metropolitan districts:** common in newer neighborhoods, and they add to your property tax bill. We build them into your payment from the start.
- **HOAs are common,** especially in newer communities and mountain towns. Dues count in your payment and your qualifying.
- **Title companies handle closings,** and Colorado's state documentary fee is tiny.
- **Mountain and wildfire-area homes** can take longer to insure and appraise. Start early.
- **Military families:** with Fort Carson, the Air Force Academy, and Space Force bases around Colorado Springs, VA loans are a big part of what we do here.

## Where in Colorado do we help?

Statewide, including **Denver and the metro area, Colorado Springs, Boulder, Fort Collins, Pueblo, Grand Junction,** and the mountain communities, by phone and video, with closings at a local title company.

## Is there down payment help in Colorado?

Yes. CHFA offers statewide grants and second-loan assistance. See [down payment assistance in CA, TX, FL, and CO](/answers/down-payment-assistance-ca-tx-fl-co).
