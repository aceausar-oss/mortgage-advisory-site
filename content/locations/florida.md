## What's different about a mortgage in Florida?

- **Documentary stamp tax on the mortgage:** $0.35 per $100 of the loan amount, paid at closing.
- **Intangible tax:** 0.2% of the loan amount, also paid at closing.
- **Homestead exemption and Save Our Homes:** file for your homestead exemption to lower the taxable value of your primary home and cap how fast its assessment can rise.
- **Insurance is a big part of the payment.** Wind coverage is a must, and flood insurance is required in flood zones. We build real quotes into your numbers early.
- **Condos need a closer look.** Newer safety and reserve rules mean some condo buildings aren't eligible for certain loans right now, so we check the building before you fall in love with the unit.
- **Title insurance rates are set by the state.**

## Example scenario (illustrative)

A retired couple from Ohio sells their home and wants to move near their grandkids in Sarasota. With a HECM for Purchase, they buy a $420,000 home using about half the price from their sale, keep the rest of their savings, and have no monthly mortgage payment. They keep paying taxes, insurance, and upkeep, and they file for their homestead exemption right away.

## Our take

Florida is where a lot of people plan their retirement, and a reverse mortgage for purchase is one of the most underused tools for it. Just get the insurance numbers early; in Florida, they can matter as much as the rate.
