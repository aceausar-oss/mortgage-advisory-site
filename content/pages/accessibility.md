We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, or other assistive technology.

## Our goal

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. That includes:

- Text and buttons with strong color contrast
- Pages that work with a keyboard alone, with visible focus outlines
- Descriptive labels for images, charts, and form fields
- Moving content (like our reviews row) that pauses on hover or focus, has a pause button, and stays still if your device is set to reduce motion
- Pages that still work with JavaScript turned off

## Known limitations

Our booking calendar is provided by a third-party scheduling service, and parts of it may not fully meet these standards. If you have any trouble booking, call us and we'll schedule for you.
