## What this means

California law gives residents the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information. We don't do either, so there's nothing to opt out of today. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) first and honor your choice.

## Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of sale and sharing.
