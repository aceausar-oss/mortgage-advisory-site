## Why do investors hit a wall with conventional loans?

Conventional loans backed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac generally cap investors at **10 financed properties**, including the home you live in. Each additional property also needs more reserves and full income documents, so it gets harder with every purchase.

## How do DSCR loans help?

- **No set property cap** at most lenders
- **Each property qualifies on its own rent**, so one more rental doesn't hurt your personal debt-to-income
- **Reserves** are often based on the property you're buying, not your whole portfolio (it varies by program)

## Do lenders limit their exposure?

Some do. A lender may cap the **number of loans** or the **total dollars** it will lend to one borrower. Working with a lender that has access to several DSCR programs keeps you from getting stuck.

## What about financing several properties at once?

Some investors use a **blanket or portfolio loan** that covers several properties under one loan. These are usually commercial-style loans with different terms. For most investors, separate DSCR loans on each property are simpler and easier to refinance or sell one at a time.

If you're planning to own more than 10 rentals, build your financing plan now, not when you hit the wall. I'll map out which properties stay conventional and which move to DSCR so your next purchase is never on hold.
