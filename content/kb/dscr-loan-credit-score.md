## How does credit affect a DSCR loan?

Credit is one of the biggest drivers of your **rate** and **down payment**:

| Credit score | What to expect |
|---|---|
| 740 and up | Best pricing and the lowest down payment options |
| 680 to 739 | Solid options, slightly higher rate |
| 620 to 679 | Fewer programs; usually more down and a higher rate |
| Under 620 | Very limited; often better to fix credit first |

These are general ranges. Each program sets its own.

## Do late payments matter?

Yes, especially **recent mortgage late payments**. Many programs want a clean mortgage history for the last 12 months. Older issues like a past foreclosure or bankruptcy usually need a waiting period, often several years.

## How can I raise my score before I apply?

- **Pay credit card balances down** below about 30% of the limit, ideally lower
- **Don't open new credit** in the months before you apply
- **Fix errors** on your credit report
- **Ask me for a rapid rescore**: if you pay something down, your lender can often update your score in days instead of waiting a month
