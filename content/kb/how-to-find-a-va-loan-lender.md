## What should I ask a VA lender?

1. **How many VA loans do you close?** Lenders who do them every week run into fewer surprises.
2. **Can you pull my Certificate of Eligibility (COE)?** An experienced VA lender can usually get it through the VA system the same day.
3. **What happens if the VA appraisal comes in low?** They should be able to explain the Tidewater process and how to request a reconsideration of value.
4. **Am I exempt from the funding fee?** They should check your COE for a disability exemption *before* they quote you.
5. **How do seller credits work on this offer?** A VA pro knows the 4% concession cap and what doesn't count toward it.
6. **Can you do a VA streamline refinance (IRRRL) later?** Good to know if rates drop after you buy.
7. **What are all of your fees?** Ask for the full Loan Estimate, not just a rate.

## What are the red flags?

- Telling you a VA loan is a "huge disadvantage" and pushing you to conventional without a real reason for your situation
- Not knowing the VA fee rules, the funding fee exemption, or how to get your COE
- Quoting a rate with no Loan Estimate showing points and fees
- Saying "VA requires a minimum credit score." It doesn't; each lender sets its own.

## Should I go local or online?

Either can work. What matters most is VA experience, how quickly they respond, and a clear Loan Estimate. A lender who knows your local market can also help when an appraisal gets tricky or a listing agent is nervous about a VA offer. Whoever you choose, compare offers from the same day, for the same loan.

## Example scenario (illustrative)

A veteran in Los Angeles asks three lenders the questions above. One isn't sure whether the veteran's disability rating removes the funding fee, and another only gives a rate. The third pulls the COE that day, confirms the exemption, and sends a full Loan Estimate. That's an easy decision, and the veteran asks the other two to match it in writing.
