## How do the programs compare?

| | FHA-insured HECM | HomeSafe Second | Jumbo lump sum (HomeSafe Standard) | Jumbo line of credit (HomeSafe Select) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Lump sum, line of credit that grows, monthly payments, or a mix | Fixed-rate second mortgage behind the current first | Fixed-rate lump sum | Adjustable-rate line of credit |
| Minimum age (CA, FL, CO / TX) | 62 / 62 | 55 / 62 | 55 / 62 | 55 / 62 |
| Current mortgage | Paid off at closing | **Stays in place** | Paid off at closing | Paid off at closing |
| Size | Home value counted up to FHA's limit ($1,249,125 in 2026) | Up to about $1 million | Up to about $4 million; usually $200,000 minimum | Up to about $4 million; usually $200,000 minimum |
| FHA insurance | Yes | No | No | No |

Amounts depend on age, home value, and rates: the older the youngest borrower, the more is available. Programs and ages vary by state. Run a client's numbers with our [reverse mortgage calculator](/reverse-calculator), or book a case review.

## Which clients fit an FHA-insured HECM?

Clients 62 or older who:

- Are finding it hard to **cover essential expenses**, or haven't saved much for retirement
- Are **spending down savings** at a pace that puts the plan at risk
- Want to **remove a mortgage payment**, or worry about keeping up with taxes and insurance
- Want a **younger spouse** to be able to stay in the home

**What you'll hear:** "I'm not covering my basics every month." "My spouse is under 62, and I want them to stay in the house."

## Which clients fit HomeSafe Second?

Clients 55 or older (62 in Texas) who:

- Have a **low-rate first mortgage** they want to keep
- Don't want to **sell investments in a down market**
- Need cash for **higher-interest debt** or home improvements, without a new monthly payment

**What you'll hear:** "I don't want to touch my investments right now." "I have a great rate and don't want to lose it." "I can't take on another payment." See [HomeSafe Second vs. a HELOC](/answers/reverse-mortgage-second-vs-heloc).

## Which clients fit a jumbo reverse mortgage?

Clients with **high-value homes**, or condos that aren't FHA-approved, who:

- Want a **large lump sum**, for example to buy a rental property, build an ADU, or reduce what they draw from investments (**HomeSafe Standard**)
- Want a **line of credit** for future needs, so they can sell investments only when markets are favorable (**HomeSafe Select**)
- Don't want to pay **FHA mortgage insurance**

See [jumbo reverse mortgages above the FHA limit](/answers/jumbo-reverse-mortgage-high-value-home).

## Where in my book should I look first?

- Clients who **just turned 55 or 62** and own their home
- Clients with a **mortgage payment still running into retirement**
- Clients drawing from investments faster than planned, or worried about **sequence risk**
- Clients who looked at a reverse mortgage years ago and passed. Today's options are broader.
