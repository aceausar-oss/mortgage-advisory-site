## How is the ratio calculated on a multi-unit?

Add up the rent from **every unit**, then divide by the full monthly payment:

- Unit A: $1,800
- Unit B: $1,750
- Unit C: $1,700
- **Total rent: $5,250 ÷ payment $4,700 = DSCR 1.12**

Rent comes from the current leases or the appraiser's market rent for each unit.

## What if one unit is vacant?

Many programs use the appraiser's **market rent** for a vacant unit, but some reduce it or want a lease in place. Tell me up front so we pick the right program.

## Can I live in one of the units?

Not with a DSCR loan; it's for investment properties only. If you want to live in one unit, look at an **FHA, VA, or conventional** loan for owner-occupied 2 to 4 unit properties, which can need much less down.

## What about 5 or more units?

Five units and up is considered **commercial multifamily**, which uses different loans and underwriting. Some DSCR lenders do 5 to 8 units under a separate program. I'll tell you which way to go.

## Our take

Small multi-units are one of my favorite investor properties: one roof, one insurance policy, several rents. Make sure each unit's rent is realistic and the building's repairs are in your budget, and I'll handle the financing.
