## Why do investors use an LLC?

Investors often hold rentals in an LLC to keep each property's business separate and for liability planning. Talk with your attorney or CPA about whether it's right for you; that part is their call, not mine.

## What documents will the lender need?

- **Articles of organization** (or incorporation)
- **Operating agreement** showing who owns the LLC
- **EIN letter** from the IRS
- **Certificate of good standing** from your state
- **Signer authority**, if more than one member

## Do I still have to personally guarantee the loan?

Usually, yes. Most DSCR lenders ask the owners (often anyone with 20% or more) to **personally guarantee** the loan, and they look at your personal credit.

## Can I move a property I already own into an LLC?

Sometimes, but be careful: transferring a property out of your name can trigger the **due-on-sale clause** on a regular mortgage. A common path is to refinance into a DSCR loan directly in the LLC's name. Talk with me and your attorney before you deed anything over.
