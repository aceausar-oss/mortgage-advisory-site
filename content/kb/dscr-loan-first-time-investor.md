## Do I need landlord experience?

Not always. Many programs accept first-time investors, but some add conditions:

- **A larger down payment**, often 25%
- **A ratio of 1.0 or higher** (no programs that allow lower ratios)
- **More reserves**
- **No short-term rentals** on your first property, in some programs

## Does it matter if I rent the home I live in?

Some programs treat first-time investors who **don't own their own home** more strictly. If you rent where you live, tell me up front so we pick the right program.

## Is a DSCR loan the best first rental loan?

Not always. If your income is easy to document and your debt-to-income is low, a **conventional investment loan** may cost less. DSCR wins when your tax returns understate your income or your debt-to-income is already stretched. I'll compare both.

## What should a first-time investor check before buying?

- **Real rent:** look at what similar homes actually rent for, not just asking prices
- **Insurance and taxes:** get real quotes; they make or break the ratio
- **Repairs and vacancy:** set aside money for both
- **Property management:** know what it costs if you won't manage it yourself
