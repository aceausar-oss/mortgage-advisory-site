## Is there a minimum credit score for a VA loan?

Not from VA. VA asks lenders to decide whether you're a satisfactory credit risk, and each lender sets its own minimum score. Many land somewhere around **580 to 620**. That's why shopping lenders matters so much if your score is on the lower side.

## Can I get a VA loan after bankruptcy?

Often, yes, and usually sooner than with a conventional loan:

- **Chapter 7:** generally **2 years after the discharge date**.
- **Chapter 13:** you may qualify after **12 months of on-time plan payments**, with the **trustee's permission** to take on the new loan.

Lenders will also want to see that you've rebuilt your credit since then: on-time payments, no new late payments, and a steady income. If the bankruptcy was caused by something outside your control, like a job loss or a medical crisis, explain it. That context helps.

## Do collections stop me from getting a VA loan?

Not automatically. Lenders look at how much, how old, what kind (medical collections are often treated differently), and whether your recent history is clean. Sometimes you'll need to pay off or set up payments on certain accounts before closing. Ask your lender to review your credit report with you before you pay anything off, because paying the wrong account at the wrong time can actually drop your score temporarily.

## How can I boost my score before I apply?

- Pay every bill on time from here on out. Recent history counts the most.
- Get credit card balances low, ideally under 30% of the limit, and even lower if you can.
- Don't open new credit or finance a car while you're buying a home.
- Dispute any errors on your credit report.

## Example scenario (illustrative)

A Navy veteran in San Diego had a Chapter 7 discharged 26 months ago after a medical crisis. Their score is 604, with clean payments since the bankruptcy. One lender's cutoff is 620, so they're declined. A second lender's cutoff is 580, and the underwriter reviews the letter explaining the bankruptcy along with 2 years of on-time rent. The veteran is approved for a VA loan.
