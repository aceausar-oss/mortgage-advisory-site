## What's the typical range?

| Situation | Typical down payment |
|---|---|
| Strong credit, ratio of 1.0 or higher | About 20% |
| Average credit, or a 2 to 4 unit property | About 25% |
| Ratio under 1.0, lower credit, or short-term rental | 25% to 30% or more |

A few programs go to 15% down for very strong files, but they usually cost more in rate.

## Can I use a gift or a HELOC for the down payment?

- **Gifts:** many DSCR programs don't allow gift money for the down payment, or limit it. Check with me first.
- **Equity from your home:** yes, many investors pull cash from their own home with a HELOC or cash-out refinance and use it for the down payment. The new payment on your home counts against your budget, so plan for it.
- **Business funds:** often allowed with access to the account.

## What else do I need cash for?

- **Closing costs:** commonly 2% to 5% of the loan amount, plus any points you choose
- **Reserves:** usually 3 to 12 months of the property's payment, left in the bank after closing

## Example (illustrative)

An investor in Colorado Springs buys a $400,000 rental. With 20% down ($80,000), his ratio is 1.05. With 25% down ($100,000), the payment drops and the ratio rises to 1.15, which gets him a better rate. He runs both numbers and picks 25% down because the monthly savings pay back the extra cash in under five years.
