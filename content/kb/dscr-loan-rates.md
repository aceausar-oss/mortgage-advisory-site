## Why do DSCR loans cost more?

- They're **Non-QM loans**, so they're held or sold to private investors instead of Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac
- The lender is relying on the **property's rent**, not your personal income
- They're **investment properties**, which carry more risk than the home you live in

## What moves my rate the most?

| Factor | Lower rate | Higher rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Credit score** | 740+ | Below 680 |
| **Down payment** | 25%+ | 20% or less |
| **DSCR ratio** | 1.25+ | Below 1.0 |
| **Prepayment penalty** | Longer (for example 5 years) | Shorter or none |
| **Property** | Single-family, long-term rental | Short-term rental, condo, 2 to 4 units |
| **Loan size** | Mid-size | Very small or very large |
| **Payment type** | Fully amortizing | Interest-only |

## Can I buy the rate down?

Yes. You can pay **discount points** at closing to lower your rate. Whether it's worth it depends on how long you'll keep the loan. I'll show you the break-even month.

## Why don't you list DSCR rates on the site?

Because a real rate depends on all the factors above, and rates move daily. A posted number would be misleading for most people. Call or chat for today's rates and I'll price your actual scenario.

## Our take

Don't shop DSCR loans on the lowest advertised rate. Compare the full picture: rate, points, prepayment penalty, and closing costs. That's where the real difference between lenders shows up.
