## What's on the checklist?

**About you**
- Photo ID
- Signed application and credit authorization

**Your money**
- **Two months of bank statements** (or more) showing your down payment, closing costs, and reserves
- Explanation of any large deposits

**The property**
- **Purchase contract** (or your current mortgage statement on a refinance)
- **Lease** if it's already rented; on a purchase, the appraiser's rent estimate is often enough
- **Insurance quote** (landlord or rental dwelling policy)
- **HOA contact info**, if any
- For short-term rentals: **booking history** or the program's market report

**If you're closing in an LLC**
- Articles of organization, operating agreement, EIN letter, and certificate of good standing

## What you don't need

- Personal tax returns
- W-2s or pay stubs
- Employment verification
- A personal debt-to-income calculation

## How fast can a DSCR loan close?

With a clean file and a quick appraisal, many DSCR loans close in about **3 to 4 weeks**. The appraisal and insurance are usually the slowest parts, so order them early.
