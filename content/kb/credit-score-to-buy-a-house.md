## What's the minimum score for each loan?

| Loan | Typical minimum score |
|---|---|
| FHA | 580 with 3.5% down; 500 to 579 with 10% down (many lenders set their own floor, often 580 to 620) |
| Conventional | About 620 at most lenders |
| VA | No VA minimum; lenders often look for about 580 to 620 |
| Non-QM | Varies by program |

See [VA credit scores and bankruptcy](/answers/va-loan-credit-score-bankruptcy) for veterans.

## Can I buy a house with bad credit?

Often, yes, especially with FHA, which is built for buyers who are rebuilding. Lenders look at the whole picture: recent on-time payments, your debt compared with your income, your savings, and any explanation for past problems. A score in the low 600s with a year of clean payments usually beats a 680 with recent late payments.

## How much does my score change the cost?

A lot on conventional loans: **both the rate and the PMI are priced by score**, so moving from 660 to 720 can save real money every month. FHA pricing is less sensitive to score, which is why FHA often wins for lower scores. See [FHA vs. conventional](/answers/fha-vs-conventional-small-down-payment).

## How can I raise my score quickly?

- **Pay every bill on time** from today on.
- **Pay credit card balances down** below 30% of each limit, and lower if you can.
- **Don't open new accounts** or finance a car while you're buying.
- **Dispute errors** on your credit reports.
- Ask us about a **rapid rescore** once balances are paid, which can update your score in days instead of a month.
