## What do you need for a first look?

1. **Ages** of the client and any spouse, including a spouse who won't be on the loan
2. **Approximate home value**
3. **Mortgage balance** and monthly payment, and whether the rate is worth keeping
4. **State** (we lend in California, Texas, Florida, and Colorado)
5. **The goal:** remove a payment, create a standby line, fund care, bridge to Social Security, or something else

## What's helpful but optional?

- Whether the home is in a **trust**
- Type of home (single-family, condo, 2–4 units)
- Rough **income** and whether property taxes and insurance have been paid on time
- Any plans to **move** in the next few years
- Whether heirs want to **keep the home**

## What should I not send?

No names, Social Security numbers, account numbers, or documents for a case review. If the client decides to move forward, we'll collect what's needed directly and securely.

## What do I get back?

- An estimated range of what's available, after paying off any current mortgage
- The main costs, in writing
- A side-by-side with alternatives, like a HELOC, a refinance, or doing nothing
- An honest opinion on fit, including "not a fit"

## Our take

Fifteen minutes and five numbers usually tell us whether it's worth your client's time. If it isn't, I'll say so, and that's still a good use of the call.
